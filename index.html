📐 Phase 2 — Photo Measurement Review

Automatic local image-based edge suggestions appear first. If the photo background is uneven or the card is tilted, the app will flag low confidence. Review both outlines carefully; lighting, artwork, glare, and borderless cards can cause errors. This is a browser-based detection aid, not yet AI grading.

● Cyan: outside card edges● Lime: printed border

Drag any of the eight colored control points (four inward-facing corners and four side midpoints) to correct its position. Drag elsewhere to pan. Use the mouse wheel or zoom buttons to magnify. Each side remembers its own corrections.

⚠ Centering NOT VERIFIED

Automatic guide positions are suggestions, NOT measured centering. Zoom in and check all four cyan and lime edges. If the printed artwork boundary cannot be identified, keep the borderless checkbox checked.

This is a manual guide review, not independent proof of measurement accuracy. Editing or resetting a guide cancels verification.

🔎 Phase 3 — Preliminary Condition Report

Finish correcting the cyan and lime guides on BOTH sides, then analyze. Your centering estimate runs locally. After confirmation, AI also inspects both uploaded photos for visible condition issues; AI usage may incur API costs.
